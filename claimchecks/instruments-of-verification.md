# Instruments of Verification (series plan)

A running series of claim-check entries on the instruments that let science check
itself. Each entry follows the same shape: public claim, kill criteria, independent
sources, verdict, boundary. Entries feed the repo's web of physical limits.

## 01. AFM — DONE
Claim: "tip thinner than an atom." Verdict: falsified (5–10 nm vs ~0.1–0.3 nm).
Files: claimcheck-afm-0001.yaml, claimcheck-afm-0001.summary.json, AFM_TIP_CLAIM_CHECK.md.

## 02. STM (next)
Claim candidate: "the STM sees individual atoms." This one is closer to true —
quantum tunneling current really does resolve single atoms on conductive surfaces.
Good contrast with AFM: shows the series isn't just debunking.

## 03. Interferometry
Claim candidate: "LIGO measures distances 10,000x smaller than a proton."
Check the strain numbers and what "measurement" means there.

## 04. Mass spectrometry
Claim candidate: "mass spec identifies any molecule." Boundary work: isomers,
ionization limits, what it can't distinguish.

## 05. Your pick
Suggest the next instrument. Rule: it must be something with a public claim
attached and a checkable number underneath.
