FROM 2D TO 3D

The Mandelbulb

A ray-marched power-8 fractal inspired by Mandelbrot iteration. It is a compelling 3D construction, not the one uniquely canonical “true 3D Mandelbrot.”

DEMONSTRATED CONSTRUCTION

Organic appearance can emerge from iteration alone.

Rotate the field and it begins to resemble coral, lungs, roots, broccoli, or architecture. The resemblance is real; a shared physical generator is a separate claim.

TRUTH LAYERS

Keep the wonder. Label the leap.

DEMONSTRATED

Equation-level or experimental connection

Mandelbrot/Julia dynamics, finite escape times, cardioid polar equations, optical caustics, interference, and deliberate sonification mappings.

STRUCTURAL ANALOGY

Measured resemblance, different generator

Neurons, lightning, trees, lungs, rivers, rose windows, and other branching or symmetric systems can be fractal-like without obeying z²+c.

PHILOSOPHICAL

Interpretation, metaphor, worldview

“All things are one thing” and “the Mandelbrot is the code of reality” are powerful framing ideas—not conclusions produced by this finite experiment.

Math may be the language we use to describe recurring structure. This lab tests the grammar without pretending every rhyme is the same sentence.
